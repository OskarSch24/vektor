# Database Studio – vollständige Bildprompts

## 01-Strata

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE finished visual concept for the macOS application Database Studio.
Art direction taken from a locally inspected Cortex icon: a freestanding sculptural matte-white object, broad gently curved low-poly facets, almost porcelain or finely carved white mineral, soft dimensional shading with pearl-white highlights and cool pale-gray recesses, exceptionally clean silhouette, luxurious restrained studio lighting from upper left. It should look like a carefully designed 3D app icon, not a line icon or clipart.
Composition: square 1024x1024 image, solid pure black background edge to edge. One isolated object centered and large, occupying approximately 66% of image width and 64% of image height. Modest three-quarter view, orthographic or very long lens, aesthetically balanced. No horizon, no floor, no visible plinth, no rounded-square background tile, no app screenshot, no typography, no letters or labels, no watermark.
Critical exclusions: NO brain, no anatomical or organic brain-like shapes, no network dots and connecting lines, no triangle of nodes, no classic cylindrical database barrel, no blue or colored gradients, no glass, no metallic chrome, no circuitry. Bold simple geometry should remain legible at small app-icon sizes.
Subject: STRATA. Exactly three thick horizontal rounded-square slabs stacked with calm deliberate narrow dark gaps. Each slab is a sculpted white porcelain wafer with generous smooth bevels and a few broad faceted surfaces, like three architectural data layers. Squarish outline with softly rounded corners, NOT circular discs. Slabs are aligned on the same vertical axis, with the middle layer translated just a little to the right to make the silhouette distinctive. View from slightly above, rotated about 30 degrees to reveal the top plane and two sides. The whole stack forms a compact substantial object about as wide as it is tall, NOT a tall tower. Make it tactile, iconic and elegant, visually suggesting stored and ordered data.
```
