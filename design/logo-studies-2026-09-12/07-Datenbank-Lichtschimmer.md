# Database Studio – minimaler Lichtschimmer

Glatte Datenbankform mit einem sehr zurückhaltenden Helligkeitsverlauf von oben im nahezu schwarzen Hintergrund. Keine sichtbare Lichtquelle und kein Lichtkegel. Die Beleuchtung des Objekts wurde im Prompt ausdrücklich beibehalten.

![Datenbank mit minimalem Lichtschimmer](07-Datenbank-Lichtschimmer.png)

Mit dem integrierten Imagegen-Werkzeug aus `06-Datenbank-Glatt.png` erstellt.

## Vollständiger Bildprompt

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit, background lighting adjustment.
Edit the supplied image with one extremely restrained adjustment to ONLY the dark background. The user wants an almost imperceptible top-down brightness fade, as if a very diffuse light source were a kilometer above the image, far outside the frame.
Keep the database sculpture exactly as shown: three smooth satin-white circular cylinder tiers, unchanged shape, proportions, position, scale, elliptical top, bevels, two grooves, surface brightness, existing highlights and shadows. Do not relight, brighten or darken the object at all.
BACKGROUND ONLY: keep it visually black. Add a very broad, extraordinarily faint neutral near-black gradient from the top edge downward. The top center should be just barely lighter than the surrounding black, approximately RGB 24,25,26 at its absolute brightest, smoothly fading toward RGB 16,17,18 in the lower half. Only about eight 8-bit brightness levels separate the brightest and darkest background. These are near-black values, NOT gray. The fade must be almost invisible and perceived only on close inspection. Spread it softly over the entire upper area with a huge feather radius and very mild falloff toward both upper corners. The imagined source is far above the canvas: no local hotspot anywhere. Transition smoothly back to the existing near-black around mid-height. Absolutely no obvious light effect.
CRITICAL: no light beam, no cone, no ray, no shaft, no visible spotlight, no circular halo, no luminous patch behind the object, no bloom, no haze, no fog, no top rim light on the sculpture, no blue or colored tint, no texture addition, no banding. Never turn the upper background medium gray. The viewer's first impression must still be a clean black background. The second, close look should reveal only the faintest difference in background tone at the top.
Preserve the square image, all object edges and original framing. Fully opaque image, no transparency, no text, no additional elements. Make the minimal background change requested and nothing else.
```
